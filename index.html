Visual System & Craft

Built on All Core Principles of Design

Every layout decision, typographic scale, and color pairing in this interface was engineered to maximize human comprehension, accessibility, and aesthetic harmony.

01. Visual Hierarchy SCALE

Expressive Plus Jakarta Sans display headlines guide the eye naturally from the mission statement to the primary classification readout and secondary confidence bars.

02. Contrast & Color Theory WCAG AA+

Crisp alabaster surfaces (#F8FAFC) paired with deep obsidian ink (#0F172A), royal indigo primary actions, and emerald accents ensure effortless readability.

03. Asymmetric & Radial Balance 7:5 RATIO

An asymmetric 7-to-5 column split balances visual weight between the live sensor viewport on the left and dense telemetry data on the right.

04. Strict Grid Alignment 12-COL

All sections anchor to a unified max-w-7xl 12-column responsive grid, creating clean vertical axis lines across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

05. Proximity & Whitespace GROUPING

Related controls (camera start, stop, mirror) are grouped tightly together while generous negative space separates distinct functional zones to prevent cognitive overload.

06. Repetition & Rhythm TOKENS

Consistent 16px card border radii, cohesive icon stroke weights, and standardized micro-interactions establish a predictable visual rhythm across the entire page.
